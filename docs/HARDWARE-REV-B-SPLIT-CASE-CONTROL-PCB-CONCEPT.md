# Rev B alternative — front electronics carrier + removable rear clamshell

**26 October 2026. PROPOSED architecture only; not an approved manufactured Rev B case, designed PCB, ordered BOM or changed CAD.** User suggested moving away from a restrictive bottom service hatch: mount the screen and hardware controls together on a PCB/carrier assembly, then install a separate rear shell via clips/hooks/slide. This is an important alternative to the currently unsolved rear-hatch insertion trajectory. Treat *assembly as one unit* as the goal, not an assumption that the purchased screen PCB can simply be soldered or screwed to a new controller PCB.

## Existing selected appearance and validated physical facts to preserve

- Same **156 mm width**, **55° face**, **R5 exterior**, **83.093 mm depth**, **106.107 mm height**, target 800×480 Waveshare **ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-4.3 touch SKU 25948**, physically rotated 180° to put power USB-C on **user's left**.
- Three MX media keys at face X **31.05, 61.05, 91.05 mm** with V=91 mm (30 mm pitch); low resting cap face height **4 mm**. Volume encoder centre X=138,V=44 mm, tentative 15.1 mm ridged aluminium knob; **encoder press switches PC/Wi-Fi control mode**. These user selections remain unchanged by the proposed enclosure seam.
- The 2023 manufacturer STEP includes the mounted screen's onboard ESP32-S3 module, PCB, four ~98×60 mm mounting positions and ports. It is **not** a bare interchangeable LCD requiring a new ESP32 controller.
- On Windows, the real **693 solid** manufacturer STEP registered to the existing V2 case in nominal final seated orientation (3.25 mm assumed glass recess) was checked against the current unmodified case: **693/693 exact model-solid intersections, no overlapping volumes >0.05 mm³**. This does not prove positive clearance, plug clearance, installation trajectory, fastener side, tolerances or physically correct revised board variant.
- Three *sampled* attempted extractions in the old closed case collided after only 3.5 mm vertical descent (with or without ~1 mm forward shift / +1.5° pitch). This motivates questioning the **service opening topology**, not blindly enlarging any aperture. A separate face-normal withdrawal trial was already committed, not yet user tested at time of this proposal.
- The original hook-and-click V2 **underside hatch** and previous screw-on/slider studies are historical baseline artifacts; if adopting a rear-half shell, the small bottom hatch would normally be **deleted in a separate NEW variant**, not awkwardly retained by default. **Never overwrite the existing accepted baseline STEP or generator.**

## Recommended assembly architecture (preferred first investigation)

**Structural printed front carrier/fascia, one small custom input-control PCB, separate original Waveshare screen, and removable rear shell.**

1. **Front structural face and carrier.** A single front subassembly, retaining the accepted look, contains the screen viewing window, three recessed keycap wells, volume knob hole, internal display ledge/standoffs and fastening receivers. A printed removable support plate/inner rib can be added behind the front if needed. Front loads are reacted through case ribs/plates, not through unsupported PCB solder joints.
2. **Existing Waveshare screen is mounted to the front carrier** at its own nominal 98×60 four fixing centres. Its screw insertion/service direction is from the now-open rear side; verify whether 2023 standoff threads/screw heads face as modelled. Keep accessible BOOT/RESET and USB port, touch/display FPC keepouts, antenna air space and any cable bend radii.
3. **One custom input PCB**, provisionally under the MX buttons and encoder. The three mechanical switches can solder to appropriately dimensioned MX footprints (if selected part supports PCB mounting) while a matching ~1.5 mm structural MX retention plate takes repeated downward key forces. Secure the encoder's bushing mechanically with its nut to a correctly dimensioned panel; do not load its switch solder pads with push/rotational forces. A PCB spanning the entire front under the screen is **an optional variant**, not yet recommended without verifying: (a) PCB-to-screen depth and standoff height; (b) MX cap 4 mm rest/4 mm travel; (c) encoder shaft and nut travel; (d) clearance to the 693 manufacturer solids; (e) fastener access. Mounting screen directly to a custom PCB may require reinforced fixing holes, spacers and a carrier, and could force key/knob far behind the face.
4. **Electronics**: six input signals = 3 MX + encoder quadrature A/B + encoder press. The screen ESP32-S3 remains the ONLY MCU. Prototype choice A is the previously selected separate Waveshare AW9523B SKU 22132 breakout connected to the custom input PCB, with six safely biased/protected input channels. Later custom PCB choice B can integrate the AW9523B IC, verified address straps, reset bias/timing, pullups/protection, bus voltage and connector; requires a completely reviewed schematic and PCB layout. The source Waveshare touchscreen exposes a 4-contact HY2.0 I²C interface; the separately selected AW9523B breakout has a **different six-contact port**, so do NOT assume a plug-and-play harness. Confirm external bus voltage and signal pin ordering before connecting.
5. **Preassemble on workbench**: install/inspect MX keys, encoder and their PCB/retainer; screw screen to printed front carrier (not automatically to PCB); install expander/harness; verify all 6 inputs, USB service and backlight/touch display; only then close the rear shell.
6. **Removable rear half/tub**: covers the back, top/side regions and base. Concept seam follows the perimeter **behind** front face, kept off top visible display/key surfaces. Try **two rigid hidden upper/forward locating hooks plus two accessible lower/rear release latches**, pressing the rear shell into place after a short locating motion. This is less demanding than sliding a fully fitted half-shell along a long rail, which could catch on the rear of the display/knob wiring. The rear shell should not carry screen fixing screws or distort its glass.
7. Provide a finger-operated *hidden* release accessible from the underside/back; shell removal exposes the entire electronics subassembly. Prototype printed clips with a short test coupon before any finished case. If a reliable zero-screw joint cannot be justified, **two concealed service screws underneath** are a safer temporary engineering fallback than inaccessible clips, without compromising the top/front appearance.
8. **External USB-C power port** remains on user's left and must line up with the original rotated screen's power socket. Provide plug body/bend/strain relief clearances and check that the rear-half seam does not cut through a thin, weakened USB port frame.

## New verification gates before selecting a mechanical design

1. **Side cross-section and collision**: import real manufacturer model and draw front carrier, control board, exact MX/encoder envelopes and a *detached* rear tub. Determine the **front face-to-PCB depth**, full 4mm key travel, encoder nut/service access and rear space. Preserve 156mm R5 silhouette where possible.
2. **Screw accessibility**: show exact Waveshare standoff thread axis/screw head side, count, usable driver approach with rear off. New fastening bosses must not touch capacitive glass, FPC, SD/BOOT/RESET or antenna keepouts.
3. **PCB architecture**: decide one board carrying 3 MX + encoder versus separate tiny key/encoder boards; if one, route all six contacts and common GND to AW9523 safely, identify connector and cable service loop. Prototype first with breakout; don't fabricate unreviewed custom AW9523 safety circuit.
4. **Rear joint geometry**: pick seam line that preserves front appearance, screen structural support, curved R5 outer edges and USB-C access. Validate assembly movement, tab print grain/strain, retention when lifted, release without tools and clip endurance. Front must be fully supported with rear removed.
5. **Assembly walkthrough**: exploded STEP of front frame + original screen + trial controls PCB + rear tub, hand/screwdriver collision checks, replaceable inputs, unplugging I²C/power and a servicing plan. Do not model fitted connectors from guessed socket rectangles.
6. **Bench hardware**: validate exact purchased screen revision, bus 3.3V, AW9523 default-output/reset network, encoder pulses while screen updates, mechanical-switch footprints, material tolerances before ordering fabricated PCB or printing a full finished shell.
